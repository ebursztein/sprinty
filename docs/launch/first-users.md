# First ten users

This is a proposed recruiting and feedback process, not a list of contacted users. Select people and send invitations yourself, or explicitly authorize a message with its recipients and channel.

## Start with real work

Aim for a mix: three website/design projects, three research/writing projects, and four projects involving parallel agents. These are a starting allocation, not a qualification rule. Prefer people who already have work to do and can describe their current process.

Offer to help them connect their client and complete one useful task. Let them choose their project; do not ask them to star the repository as a condition of help.

## Invitation draft

I'm testing Sprinty, an open-source tool for organizing work across AI agents and sessions. Do you have a website, research, design, or multi-agent project you could try it on? I'd help you get set up and would mainly like to understand what feels useful or gets in the way. No need to publish your work. Here's the short demo: https://ebursztein.github.io/sprinty/demo.html

## Questions

1. What were you trying to accomplish, and how did you coordinate it before?
2. Could you install and see the dashboard? Where did you get stuck?
3. Did breaking the work into tasks help, or add unnecessary steps?
4. After changing sessions, what context did you still have to repeat?
5. Did another agent work on the same plan? Were ownership and handovers clear?
6. What convinced you a task was finished? What evidence was missing?
7. Did you choose to use Sprinty again on another day? Why or why not?

## Minimal manual tracker

Keep participant names, project details, and private feedback outside this public repository. Use anonymous cohort labels here only if participants consent.

| Participant alias | Project type | Setup worked | First task completed | Sprint finished | Used again | Main friction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Treat setup success and a recorded completed task as separate outcomes. Record time only if the participant supplies it; do not invent improvement percentages. Ask separately before publishing a quote, screenshot, or identifiable story.
